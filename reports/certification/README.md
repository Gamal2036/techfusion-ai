# Reports — Certification

> **Purpose:** Design quality certification evidence under TG-3.
> **Owner:** Design Quality — VP of Product Design
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-01

---

## What belongs here

- Certification records for surfaces evaluated under TG-3
- TG-3 scores, gate results, and certification decisions
- Evidence that a surface is Production-ready before release

## Process

- Use the `templates/certification.md` template
- Target score: **95+**; below **90** is **Rejected** (TG-CORE Section 10)
- No surface enters Production without certification under TG-3
